Flow Path Summary
How to use
  1. Pick a material, rating and diameter under Pipe Specification. This sets the active drawing colour.
  2. Press Draw Pipe (P). Click the map to drop nodes, click an existing node to branch a tee (up to three connections). Hold Shift to lock a segment to 15 degree steps. Right click finishes a run, Esc returns to the hand.
  3. With the hand tool, click an end node to set its elevation and mark it Flow In or Flow Out with a flow rate. Right click an intermediate node to mark it a Point of Interest, a deliberate break in the flow.
  4. The tool letters the consequential nodes, balances the flow through the network, and works out each pipe velocity from the catalogue bore.
  5. Use the Callouts panel at the top left of the map, and the per element table under the map, to choose exactly what each callout shows.
  6. Press Copy Map Image to place an A3 ready figure on the clipboard.
Good to know
  • Drag a node to move it. Drag a node callout to reposition it for a clean printout. Right click a pipe to reverse its flow direction or delete it.
  • Deleting an inline node between two identical pipes merges them. If the two specs differ it leaves a gap.
  • Pan by left dragging with the hand, or by holding the middle mouse button in any tool. Zoom with the wheel.
  • Pipe lengths follow the map unless you enter a manual length. Auto elevations come from an online terrain service.
  • Velocity uses v = Q / A, with the internal bore read from the PE SDR and carbon steel schedule charts.
  • Save writes a project file you can reopen later. Your callout choices are stored inside it.
